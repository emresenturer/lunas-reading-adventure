Tips

  • Keep it short: one day per sitting, 3 to 5 minutes. Stop while it is still fun.
  • Sit together. Repeat Luna's words with your child and point at the letters.
  • Say letter sounds, not names: "sss" for S, "mmm" for M.
  • Repeat a day any time. Replaying builds confidence and still earns stars.
  • Praise effort ("you kept trying!") more than being right.
  • Find the day's letters and words in books, signs and packaging.
  • Speech recognition is imperfect with young voices. If Luna misses a word, tap "I said it!".

Your data

No account, no tracking. Progress is saved in this browser's local storage. In some browsers (for example Chrome), speech recognition audio is processed by the browser maker's online service. Turn off the microphone above to avoid that.
